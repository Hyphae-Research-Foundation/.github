# AI-assisted contributions

AI-assisted contributions are welcome. Tools do not replace human judgment,
authorship, accountability, or project authority.

The person submitting an issue, pull request, review, document, test, or
research record is responsible for every line, claim, source, test, and
artifact, regardless of how it was created.

Material AI assistance must be disclosed in the approved tracking issue and
pull request. The contributor must understand the affected subsystem, review
the complete diff, verify generated analysis against authoritative sources,
test independently, check provenance and licensing, and explain the solution
in their own words.

AI is not an author, co-author, DCO signatory, reviewer, maintainer, or reason
to merge. Automated reviews are advisory and never count as human approval.

Do not send credentials, private vulnerabilities, personal data, unreleased
source, restricted model artifacts, or confidential records to an
unauthorized AI provider. Do not accuse a contributor of AI use based on
writing style, fluency, disability, language background, or intuition.

Project repositories may define stricter rules. The complete current policy is
published at https://research.hyphae.dev/participate/.

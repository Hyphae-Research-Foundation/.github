# Community conduct

We commit to participation free from harassment, discrimination,
intimidation, and retaliation. Be precise, respectful of boundaries, generous
with context, and critical of work without attacking people.

Do not use sexualized language or attention, threaten harm, publish private
information, sustain disruption after being asked to stop, or retaliate
against a good-faith report.

This applies in repositories, reviews, issues, Discussions, events, and
communication made in an official project role. Project maintainers may
remove content or restrict participation to protect the community. Conflicted
maintainers must recuse.

The initiative is still appointing independent confidential conduct
recipients. Do not post sensitive reports publicly. The incomplete reporting
structure is documented at https://research.hyphae.dev/participate/.

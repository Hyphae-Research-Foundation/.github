## Research infrastructure, cultivated in public

Hyphae Research Foundation is the institutional home being formed to steward
Hyphae and related open systems research. We build software whose state,
boundaries, provenance, and decisions can be examined rather than merely
trusted.

The foundation is currently an **open-source initiative in formation**. It is
not yet a separate legal or tax-exempt entity. Celiums Solutions LLC serves as
organizer, interim custodian, funder, and operator until incorporation and
documented asset transfers are complete, and as incorporator only if formation
filings name it. It does not own the future foundation.

## Projects

### [Hyphae](https://github.com/Hyphae-Research-Foundation/hyphae)

A local-first Rust data engine that unifies SQL, native structures,
lexical/vector search, WAL, MVCC, recovery, and verifiable proofs in one
binary. Public under Apache-2.0.

### Hyphae Inference

A pure C11 MoE inference engine with a memory. Hyphae records routing history
so expert placement can respond to the workload rather than starting cold.
Private development; not yet available or ready for use.

## How we work

- **Local first:** useful without a required cloud or external authority.
- **Evidence before headlines:** performance and correctness claims carry
  protocols, source identities, limitations, and retained evidence.
- **Failure is part of the contract:** ambiguous input and exhausted budgets
  are refused rather than guessed.
- **Negative results remain visible:** failed approaches and corrected claims
  stay in the research record.
- **Open means governable:** code, decisions, roles, rights, and succession
  must all be legible.

## Contributing

We use conversation-gated contributions:

> **Discuss → Approve → Build → Review**

Do not open a pull request before a maintainer approves a public tracking
issue. Early ideas belong in Discussions. Every implementation requires an
issue carrying `decision:approved`, a bounded scope, acceptance criteria, risk
classification, and a committed reviewer.

AI-assisted contributions are welcome. Material AI use must be disclosed. The
human contributor remains responsible for every line, claim, source, test, and
artifact; project-specific review rules determine the required gate.

Read the organization-wide [contribution policy](../CONTRIBUTING.md) before
proposing work. Project repositories may add stricter technical, AI, security,
and review requirements.
